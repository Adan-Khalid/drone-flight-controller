### Why the processor rail uses a buck regulator (ambient 45 °C)

| Load | AP2112K LDO: loss, junction temperature | TLV62569 buck: conduction loss, junction temperature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| typical, 0.340 A | 0.60 W, 155 °C | 0.010 W, 47 °C |
| worst, 0.556 A | 0.97 W, 224 °C | 0.027 W, 50 °C |

The AP2112K's junction limit is 150 °C, so dropping 5 V to 3.3 V in a linear regulator at the processor's current is not acceptable. The TLV62569 buck wastes far less (its switching losses are not counted above). Its feedback top resistor is 450 kΩ ideal, 453 kΩ chosen (E96) with 100 kΩ bottom, giving 3.318 V. Ripple current 0.345 A, peak 0.73 A at worst-case load, against a 3.0 A current limit.

The sensor LDO (TPS7A2033) burns only 11.5 mW.

## 8. DShot600 timing

One bit lasts 1.667 µs and a 16-bit frame 26.67 µs, so the motor-signal edges must settle in a small fraction of a microsecond (checked in KiCad's simulator).
